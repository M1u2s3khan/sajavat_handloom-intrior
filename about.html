About Us

Craftsmanship, comfort aur imaandari — yahi hamari pehchaan hai.

Hamari Kahani Purani cheezon ko naya jeevan, haathon se buni khoobsurti

Sajawat Handloom & Sofa Repair ek trusted local business hai jo handloom furnishings aur sofa repair services dono mein quality aur customer satisfaction par focus karta hai. Hamara maqsad simple hai — aapke ghar ko sundar, comfortable aur naya jaisa look dena.

Chahe aapko handloom fabric se banaye gaye curtains, cushion covers, table runners, bed covers ya custom furnishings chahiye, ya aapke purane sofa ki foam replacement, upholstery change, stitching, spring repair ya polishing karwani ho — hum har kaam ko kaariGari aur imaandari se karte hain.

Traditional handloom ki craftsmanship ko modern designs ke saath jodkar hum aise products aur services dete hain jo ghar ki beauty badhate hain. Saath hi, sofa repair ke through hum purane furniture ko naya jeevan dene mein madad karte hain.
