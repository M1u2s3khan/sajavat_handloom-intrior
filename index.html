Frequently Asked Questions

Aapke Sawaal, Hamare Jawaab

Kuch common sawaal — agar aapka sawaal nahi hai, toh WhatsApp par poochein.

Basic repair 2-3 din mein ho jaata hai. Complete restoration mein 5-7 din lagte hain. Custom premium sofa ke liye 10-15 din lag sakte hain, kyunki designer fabric order karna padta hai.

Haan! Sheher aur aas-paas ke 15 km area mein free home visit dete hain. Hum ghar aakar sofa check karte hain aur exact quote dete hain — bina kisi obligation ke.

Haan, 15 km ke area mein pickup aur delivery bilkul free hai. Iske baad bhi nominal charge lagta hai jo pehle hi bataya jaata hai.

Hamari 6-month warranty stitching, fitting aur material ki defects ko cover karti hai. Normal wear-and-tear ya misuse cover nahi hota.
Apna fabric chun sakte hain?+
Bilkul! Hum 200+ fabric options le kar aate hain — cotton, linen, velvet, leatherette aur designer prints. Aap apni pasand ka color aur design chun sakte hain.
Payment kaise karna padta hai?+
Hum cash, UPI, bank transfer aur card — sab accept karte hain. 50% advance kaam shuru karne se pehle aur baaki delivery par.
Handloom products asli hai?+
Haan, hamare saare handloom products haath se bune jaate hain — direct artisan se. Pure cotton aur natural dyes ka istemaal hota hai.

Haan! Hotels, offices, restaurants aur interior designers ke liye bulk orders karte hain. Special rates aur dedicated timeline ensure karte hain.
